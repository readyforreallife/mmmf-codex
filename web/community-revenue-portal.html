Community & Group Workshops

Live offers that do not require a school contract

Half-day workshop

$150–$200

Per attendee for a 3–4 hour session.

  • 20 attendees at $175 average = about $3,500 per event.
  • Fits church groups, youth organizations, PTSO nights, and community centers.
  • No school contract required to get started.

Full-day workshop

$200–$300

Per attendee for a 6–8 hour intensive.

  • 20 attendees at $250 average = about $5,000 per event.
  • Deeper curriculum coverage with workbook materials.
  • Strong fit for teen leadership and community development programs.
Online & Self-Paced

Offers that scale without another live event every time

Individual online course

$99–$297

Per person for self-paced access.

  • Works for parents, homeschool families, and individual learners.
  • No live facilitation required once built.
  • Creates evergreen revenue that can grow without matching time cost.

Course + coaching bundle

$500–$1,000

Per person for course access plus live accountability sessions.

  • Built for small groups of 4–6 participants.
  • Higher price point with lower volume.
  • Useful for building referrals and long-term support relationships.
Corporate & Organization Contracts

Custom engagements that grow visibility and contract revenue

Organization contract

$2,000–$5,000

Per engagement for a private organization or employer group.

  • Strong fit for nonprofits, businesses, faith organizations, and reentry programs.
  • Custom delivery for groups of up to 30.
  • Useful bridge between community workshops and larger contracts.

Speaking engagement

$500–$3,000

Per event, depending on audience size and travel.

  • Fits conferences, parent nights, and community summits.
  • Often creates follow-on workshop bookings.
  • Useful for brand visibility while building the referral network.

About the Program

Ready for Real Life Instruction and Education is a 16-week, scenario-based curriculum focused on applied decision-making, real-world communication, and character development. It is designed for school, community, and organizational settings, which makes it flexible enough to support both mission-driven delivery and layered revenue planning.
