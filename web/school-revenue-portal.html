Phase 1 — Proving It Works

Start with pilots and entry-point offers

Single class pilot

$2,500

One-time pilot price for a single class or school entry point.

  • Three pilots create a $7,500 first-year floor.
  • Low commitment for school administrators.
  • Builds testimonials, data, and proof before bigger contracts.

Self-paced online course

$99–$497

Separate individual / parent market that does not depend on licensing.

  • Useful for families, homeschool learners, and independent buyers.
  • Creates a second revenue stream outside school contracts.
  • Can run alongside pilot outreach without slowing it down.
Phase 2 — Recurring Revenue

Move from pilots into license and certification revenue

Full school license

$4,000–$6,000

Per school, per year.

  • Ten schools produces about $40K–$60K annually.
  • Creates recurring annual license revenue.
  • Includes curriculum access, pacing guides, and implementation materials.

Teacher certification

$1,500–$3,000

Per educator, one-time, through Teach the Teacher.

  • Five educators adds about $7,500–$15,000.
  • Stacks on top of the school license fee.
  • Lets schools deliver the program independently.
Phase 3 — Scale

Expand into district contracts and long-term valuation

District partnership

$10K–$30K

Per district, per year, across multiple schools.

  • Five districts can represent roughly $50K–$150K annually.
  • Scales across multiple schools in one contract.
  • Includes rollout support and training.

Outright IP sale value

$200K–$1M+

Only after real traction and repeatable revenue.

  • Pre-traction estimate: roughly $15K–$50K.
  • At 10 schools: roughly $100K–$200K.
  • At 50+ schools: $500K–$1M+ using a 2–5x annual revenue lens.

About the Program

Ready for Real Life Instruction and Education is a 16-week, scenario-based curriculum focused on applied decision-making, real-world communication, and character development. The school and district model is designed so a certified educator can deliver the program independently using instructor materials, pacing guides, scenario libraries, and student activities.
